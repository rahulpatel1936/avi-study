We are inviting PSYC*2070 students to take part in a research study on asynchronous video interviews, in which you record your answers to questions online. Employers increasingly use this interview format.

Complete a mock video interview and a short survey online, on your own computer, at a time that suits you. You can request personalized coaching and feedback on your interview responses, which is useful preparation for co-op, internship, and part-time job applications. To request it, email Rahul Patel at rpatel40@uoguelph.ca.
